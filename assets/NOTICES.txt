ok is a writing tool for Windows: one window, one .exe, and a page
drawn on a grid of character cells. The .exe holds three
typefaces, the Rust standard library, and the MinGW-w64 and GCC
runtime code it is linked with. Their notices follow, and ok
contains no other third-party code.


Gallant

in April 1982 for the Sun PROM monitor, by his own account in a
1995 Usenet post quoted in the upstream README. Jef Poskanzer
carried it into 4.4BSD, and Jens Schweikhardt extended it to
Unicode in 2025 and 2026. The source is
https://github.com/NanoBillion/gallant at commit
ed9de4dfe047322d181618ed68a485ce8fa153e3. It comes with the three
notices that follow.
