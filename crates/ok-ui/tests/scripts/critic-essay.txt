type He let me think that for about a week. Then he walked me to the one tree he had left alone on purpose, TK the year he planted it, and showed me what keeping all three looks like. The branches had split under the weight. The fruit was the size of walnuts and tasted of nothing. He did not say anything about it, which I have come to understand as a kind of teaching, the kind I got again in [[Grandfather interview]] when I asked what he would do differently and he said he would have **thinned harder**.

# Hold Ctrl for 500 ms: the say row prints its row of the table.
key ctrl+end
hold ctrl
wait 600
shot ../../../../shots/critic-hold-ctrl.png
print
release
hold alt
wait 600
shot ../../../../shots/critic-hold-alt.png
print
release
# The cursor resting after a full row: row 14 of the screen is 66 cells.
cursor 4 448
key end
shot ../../../../shots/critic-full-row-cursor.png
print
# Window loses the keyboard: the hollow box.
key ctrl+end
focus off
shot ../../../../shots/critic-unfocused.png
focus on
# Type on the heading: a change bar two rows tall.
cursor 0 13
type s
shot ../../../../shots/critic-heading-bar.png
