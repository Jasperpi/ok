# The orchard

The orchard was eleven acres when my grandfather bought it and eleven acres when he stopped farming it, and in between he never once owned a tape measure longer than his arm. He kept the trees in rows he paced out in boots, one long step and one short.

I spent the summer I was fifteen on a ladder in those rows. The job was thinning: you take off two of every three apricots while they are still green and hard, so that the one you leave has room to become something. It felt like *waste*. I filled buckets with fruit that would never ripen and carried them to the compost, and I remember thinking that a better farmer would have found a way to keep all three.
